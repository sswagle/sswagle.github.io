AI Governance

Building trustworthy, transparent and responsible artificial intelligence systems.

Introduction

AI Governance focuses on establishing principles, frameworks, and standards to ensure AI systems are trustworthy, transparent, secure, and accountable.

It enables organisations to manage AI-related risks, comply with emerging regulations, and promote responsible AI adoption.

Why AI Governance Matters

As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly integrated into business processes and critical systems, organisations need effective governance approaches to address issues related to transparency, security, privacy, safety and ethical use.

Standards and Regulatory Landscape

International standards and emerging regulatory frameworks provide guidance for organisations seeking to develop and deploy AI systems responsibly.

Key Takeaways

  • Trustworthy AI requires clear governance principles.
  • Standards help organisations manage AI risks.
  • Compliance and innovation must progress together.
  • Responsible AI adoption requires technical and organisational measures.
